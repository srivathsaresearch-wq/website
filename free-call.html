Questions about the free call

Pick a time →
Is it really free?

Yes. There's nothing to pay and we never ask for card details. If our Saturday class or membership would help you, we may mention it. There's no pressure to join.

Is this investment advice?

No. It's education. We explain how things work in general terms so you can make your own decisions. We don't tell anyone what to buy, sell or hold, and we don't review portfolios.

Why do you ask what I invest in?

So we don't spend your 30 minutes on things you already know. A rough answer is fine, and "Prefer not to say" is always an option.

What do I need for the call?

A phone or laptop with an internet connection. Open the Google Meet link from your email: in a browser on a laptop, or in the Google Meet app on a phone.

Why do you ask me to confirm the day before?

Calls are one to one, so an empty slot is time someone else could have had. If we don't hear back a few hours before the call, we free up the time. You can always book again.

Can I change or cancel?

Yes. Use the "Cancel your booking" link in your confirmation email, then pick a new time. Booking a new time with the same email also replaces your earlier one.
