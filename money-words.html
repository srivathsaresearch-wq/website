What's inside

Four places these words turn up

On your payslip

Gross and net salary, EPF, TDS, the old and new tax regime, 80C.

On your SIP statement

SIP, AMC, folio number, NAV, units, current value, absolute return, XIRR, redemption.

In a fund factsheet

Expense ratio, direct and regular plans, exit load, lock-in, benchmark, AUM, riskometer.

Everyday investing words

Inflation, compounding, diversification, KYC, demat account, capital gains.

A taste: Expense ratio. The yearly fee for running the fund, as a % of your money. It comes out of the fund, so you never see a bill.
