About the class

What will I learn?

How inflation and interest affect your money, what equity, debt and gold are, how mutual funds and index funds work, how to think about risk, the basics of costs and tax, and common mistakes. See the full outline.

Do I need money invested to join?

No. You don't need a demat account, an app or any investments to take part.

When are classes?

Every Saturday, 3:00 to 4:00 pm IST, live on Google Meet. Each class is one hour, with general Q&A.

Is it live or recorded?

Live on Google Meet. We don't share recordings.

How big is the class?

Small, on purpose. A small group means there's time for everyone's questions, and we can slow down when something isn't clear. If a Saturday fills up, we'll offer you a place in the next one.

What language is it in?

English. Sometimes we also explain things in Hindi or Kannada.

What if I miss the class?

We don't share recordings. Email us before the class starts and we'll move you to a later Saturday. You can do this once.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes. Join from the Google Meet app on your phone, or from a web browser on a laptop. You don't need to install anything on a laptop.

Advice and safety

Is the class investment advice?

No. It is education only. We explain how things work so you can make your own decisions.

Do you recommend stocks?

No. We do not name individual stocks, we don't name funds to buy, and we don't make calls on the market.

Can I lose money investing?

Yes. All investing carries risk, including loss of capital. Anyone who says otherwise is a warning sign.

Can you look at my portfolio in the class?

No. The class stays general, because looking at your own investments would be personal advice. We can teach you how to review it yourself, in class or through one-year support.

How do I spot an investment scam?

Be careful if anyone promises guaranteed or very high returns, rushes you to decide, asks you to pay into a personal account, or asks for your OTP, password or UPI PIN.

We will never do any of these.

One-year support

What is one-year support, and what does it cost?

A year of learning support after the class. Email us questions about how investing works whenever they come up, and join a monthly members' Q&A call on Google Meet. It costs ₹3,000 for 12 months and doesn't renew by itself.

It opens soon; you can register your interest.

Is one-year support investment advice?

No. Like the class, it's education. We explain how things work, and we never tell anyone what to buy, sell or hold.
