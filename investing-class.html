What the class covers

Six ideas, in the order they make sense

We start with the everyday (inflation, interest) and move to the things people get wrong.

After the class, you'll understand

  • What you actually own when you buy a mutual fund or an index fund.
  • Why different investments carry different risks, and how time and spreading your money change the picture.
  • Which costs and taxes to look for, and where to find them on a fund factsheet.
  • The warning signs of tips groups and “guaranteed” returns.

These are learning goals, not promised results. What you do with your money stays your decision.

Is it for you?

Who this class is for, and who it isn't

It's for you if

  • You earn a salary and want to understand investing before you start.
  • You have an SIP or a few funds and aren't sure what they do.
  • Money talk makes you nervous and you want it explained slowly.
  • You'd like to make your own decisions, with the basics in place.

It's not for you if

  • You want stock tips or a list of funds to buy. We don't give them.
  • You want advice on your own portfolio or situation.
  • You're looking for trading strategies or quick returns.
  • You already invest with confidence and know these basics well.

Why pay ₹199 when there's so much free content?

Free videos and apps are useful once you know what to look for. Most people starting out don't, yet. In one live hour we go through the basics in an order that makes sense, and answer your questions as we go. We don't sell funds or give tips, so we have nothing to push.

Do I need a demat account or any money to invest?

No. You don't need any account, app or money invested to join.

What if I can't attend live?

We don't share recordings. If you can't make it, email us before the class starts and we'll move you to a later Saturday. You can do this once.

How big is the class?

Small, on purpose. A small group means there's time for everyone's questions, and we can slow down when something isn't clear. If a Saturday fills up, we'll offer you a place in the next one.

Can I ask about my own investments?

You can ask how something works. We won't comment on what you personally should buy, sell or hold, because that would be advice.

Which language is the class in?

English, and sometimes Hindi or Kannada when it helps. Tell us your preference in the form and we'll keep it in mind.

How do I pay, and can I get a refund?

Online through Razorpay, including UPI, on the step after the sign-up form. Payments are non-refundable, but you can move to a later Saturday once. See our refund and cancellation policy.
