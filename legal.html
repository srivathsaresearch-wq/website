Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and how to ask us to delete it.

What we collect

  • Class sign-up: name, email, optional phone number, the class date you booked, and, if you answer them, your preferred language and “Where are you today?”.
  • One-year support (when open): name, email, optional phone number, and, if you tell us, what you would like to understand better. Register-interest forms ask only for name, email and an optional note.
  • Questions you email us: your email address and what you write, so we can answer.
  • Payments: a payment reference from Razorpay, the amount and the date. Razorpay handles the payment itself. We never receive or store card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords.
  • Newsletter: email address.
  • Contact form: name, email, topic and your message.
  • Feedback: what you write in our feedback form. We only publish a quote with your written consent, and only your first name.

What we never collect: ID numbers (such as PAN, Aadhaar or passport numbers), bank or demat account numbers, card details, passwords or OTPs. Please don't send them to us in any form or message.

Why we use it

To run the class or support you signed up for, send the Google Meet link, reminders and calendar invites, take payment, let you know once if a booking you started wasn't finished, reply to your messages, send the newsletter you asked for, and meet legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the site and classes: Google (Sheets, where sign-ups are stored, and Gmail, Calendar and Meet, for confirmations, reminders and the class link), Razorpay (payments), our email service and our hosting.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it to run your class or support and to meet legal and tax requirements, and for no more than 5 years. We then delete it. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Cookies and analytics

The site works without cookies. We count visits with Google Analytics, which uses cookies, so we ask first with a cookie notice: nothing loads unless you say yes, and saying no changes nothing else on the site. It shows us which pages are read and where people stop when signing up for a class. It is not sent your name, email or phone number. If we ever add advertising measurement (such as the Meta Pixel), the same notice will cover it.

Your rights, and how to ask for deletion

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent at any time. To ask for deletion, email info@researchbysrivathsa.com with the subject “Delete my data”, from the email address you used with us. We'll confirm when it's done. Every newsletter email also has an unsubscribe link.
