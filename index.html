Quick check

Myth or fact?

Six things people often believe about money. Tap a card to see the answer.

“You need a lot of money to start investing.”

Myth

Starting small is possible. What matters is understanding what you're buying, and the costs.

“A hot tip from a friend is research.”

Myth

A tip isn't a reason. Ask: what's the risk, the cost and the exit?

“Investments can go down as well as up.”

Fact

Every investment carries risk, including loss of capital.

“If it's guaranteed, it's safe.”

Myth

Be careful with anyone promising high, fixed or guaranteed returns.

“Tax affects what you actually keep.”

Fact

The same investment can leave you with different amounts after tax. We cover this in class.

“Small fees don't really matter.”

Myth

Fees come out every year, whether you gain or lose. Over a long time, a small difference adds up.
