About us

Most people learn investing from tips. We'd like to change that.

Why we exist

Good information shouldn't be hard to find

First-time investors in India hear a lot of noise: forwarded messages, ads, people promising quick returns. Very little of it explains how investing actually works.

We're a small research team, led by our founder, a CFA Charterholder. We spend our time reading data and reports, and we think the basics should be explained plainly, at a price most people can manage.

So we teach. We don't sell tips, we don't name stocks to buy, and we don't tell you what to do with your money. We help you understand enough to decide for yourself.

What we hold ourselves to

Four rules we teach by

We teach. We don't tip.

No stock names, no "buy now" calls, no personal advice. Education only.

We show our sources

Every number we use carries where it came from and when. If we can't source it, we don't show it.

We say when we don't know

Markets are uncertain. We'd rather tell you that than pretend otherwise.

We keep it affordable

Learning the basics shouldn't cost more than a meal out. That's why the class is ₹199.

How we think about risk

Risk is the price of growth. It's never zero.

Risk first, then returns.

Different kinds of investments go up and down by different amounts. Things that can grow more over long periods usually swing more along the way. Nothing is risk-free, and anyone who says otherwise is selling something.

We teach risk as three questions: When will you need this money? How would you feel if it fell for a while? What happens if you're wrong? Your answers matter more than any prediction.
