Questions about one-year membership

All questions →
Is this investment advice?

No. It's education, the same as the class. We explain how things work so you can make your own decisions. We don't tell anyone what to buy, sell or hold.

What can I ask?

Anything about how investing works: what a term means, how a product or a cost works, how tax applies in general, how to read a statement or a factsheet. We won't review your own portfolio or recommend products.

When is the members' call?

Once a month, live on Google Meet. We email members the date and link ahead of time. Calls are not recorded.

Does it renew automatically?

No. It runs for 12 months from the day you pay and then ends. We'll email you before it does.

What's the refund policy?

Payments are non-refundable. See our refund and cancellation policy.
